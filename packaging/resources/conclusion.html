Duette is installed

Look for the Duette icon in the menu bar at the top of the screen. If it is not there, open Duette from the Applications folder.

In the app you want to record with, for example OBS, Zoom or QuickTime, pick Duette as the microphone.

To remove Duette later, open Terminal and run:
sudo "/Library/Application Support/Duette/uninstall.sh"
